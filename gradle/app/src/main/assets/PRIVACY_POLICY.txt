APOCALYPTIC AI — PRIVACY POLICY
Effective date: October 16, 2026

ABOUT THIS POLICY
This policy describes Apocalyptic AI for Android and the data handling implemented in this app version. Apocalyptic AI is a local-model preparedness assistant. Publisher/privacy contact: raihan.sites.bd. The app does not create user accounts or operate a cloud AI inference service, advertising network, or analytics service.

WHAT THE APP STORES ON YOUR DEVICE
• Chat messages and saved conversations are stored in the app's private storage so you can reopen them. The selected on-device model processes prompts and generates replies locally.
• Checklist state, appearance and generation preferences, selected model, and download status are stored in app preferences.
• Downloaded model files are stored in app-specific storage. Android backup is disabled. You can clear conversations or remove model files from the app; uninstalling removes app-private data, subject to Android/device behavior.
• The app does not intentionally collect account details, contacts, location, camera data, or payment-card details.

VOICE FEATURES
Voice input is optional and starts only after you tap the microphone, review the in-app disclosure, and grant microphone permission. On supported Android versions, audio is passed to Android's on-device speech recognizer to produce draft text. The app does not send the recording to its own server. Recognition availability and the system service's handling depend on Android and the speech model installed on your device. Spoken replies are optional and use the Android text-to-speech engine and voice installed on your device; that engine's offline behavior and data practices are controlled by the device/provider.

NETWORK AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
Android DownloadManager downloads a model only after you choose one. The selected model host and content-delivery provider may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address, requested file, and request time; their terms and privacy policies apply. Choosing Donate opens raihan.sites.bd in an external browser, which is subject to that website's and browser's own practices. The app does not send prompts to a cloud AI inference service. Third-party model files and libraries are identified in the app's Open-source notices and project documentation.

PERMISSIONS
Internet access is used for user-requested model downloads and opening the donation website. Microphone permission is requested only for voice input. The app does not request location, contacts, camera, phone-state, or notification permissions.

RETENTION AND DELETION
Saved app conversations and preferences remain on the device until you remove them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Downloaded model files remain until removed in the app or until app data is cleared/uninstalled. Android backup is disabled. This app has no user accounts, so there is no account deletion process; deleting local conversations, removing downloads, clearing app data, or uninstalling are the available local controls.

AI AND SAFETY NOTICE
AI-generated responses can be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or unsafe. The app is an educational preparedness aid, not an emergency service and not medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice. Do not rely on it for urgent decisions. Follow local law, official emergency instructions, and qualified professional guidance; contact emergency services when appropriate. You are responsible for deciding whether and how to use or share AI output. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be limited.

CHILDREN AND CONTACT
The app is not designed as a children's service. For questions or privacy requests, contact the publisher through raihan.sites.bd.

CHANGES
This policy should be updated whenever the app's data practices change. Keep the in-app copy, public privacy-policy URL, and Google Play Data safety declarations consistent with the released version. An in-app policy does not replace the public privacy-policy URL or Play Console declarations, and no statement guarantees store approval.
