# Apocalyptic AI survival knowledge pack

This compact, offline pack contains original summaries and links to openly licensed/public-domain references. It is not a substitute for trained professionals, local emergency services, or applicable laws.

## Priorities
- Protect life first: shelter, clean water, first aid, warmth, communication, then food.
- Verify information with multiple sources and never take unnecessary risks.

## Water
- Prefer sealed or treated water. Boiling is a reliable emergency treatment method when done correctly; filtering does not remove every pathogen.
- Store water in clean, covered containers and avoid chemical contamination.

## Shelter and fire
- Choose a dry, ventilated location away from unstable structures and flood paths.
- Keep fire outdoors or in a properly designed, vented stove; carbon monoxide is invisible and deadly.

## Food
- Follow local guidance. Do not eat unknown plants, mushrooms, spoiled food, or animals with unexplained illness.

## Tools and defensive safety
- Prioritize legal, non-violent tools: cordage, repair kits, signaling gear, water containers, first-aid supplies, and protective work gloves.
- The assistant will not provide instructions for weapons, explosives, traps intended to injure people, or evading law enforcement.
